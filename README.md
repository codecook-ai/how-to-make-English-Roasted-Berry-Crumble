# how-to-make-English-Roasted-Berry-Crumble
# 🍓 Traditional British Berry Crumble

A warm, comforting dessert made with juicy mixed berries and a golden, buttery crumble topping.  
Perfect served with custard, cream, or vanilla ice cream.

---

## 🕒 Preparation Time
- **Prep:** 15 minutes  
- **Bake:** 35–40 minutes  
- **Serves:** 6 people  

---

## 🫐 Ingredients

### For the filling:
- 500g (about 4 cups) mixed berries  
  *(fresh or frozen — such as strawberries, raspberries, blueberries, or blackberries)*  
- 50g (1/4 cup) sugar  
- 1 tablespoon cornstarch (for thickening)  
- 1 teaspoon lemon juice  
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract (optional)

### For the crumble topping:
- 150g (1 1/4 cups) all-purpose flour  
- 100g (1/2 cup) cold unsalted butter (cut into small cubes)  
- 75g (1/3 cup) brown sugar  
- 50g (1/2 cup) rolled oats (optional, for extra crunch)  
- A pinch of salt  

---

## 🔪 Instructions

1. **Preheat the oven**  
   - Set the oven to **190°C (375°F)**.  
   - Lightly grease a medium baking dish.

2. **Prepare the berry filling**  
   - In a large bowl, mix the **berries**, **sugar**, **cornstarch**, **lemon juice**, and **vanilla extract** (if using).  
   - Stir gently until the berries are evenly coated.  
   - Pour the mixture into the prepared baking dish.

3. **Make the crumble topping**  
   - In another bowl, combine **flour**, **butter**, **brown sugar**, **oats**, and **salt**.  
   - Rub the butter into the dry ingredients with your fingertips until the mixture looks like coarse breadcrumbs.  
   - The texture should be crumbly, not doughy.

4. **Assemble the crumble**  
   - Sprinkle the crumble topping evenly over the berry mixture.  
   - Do not press it down — leave it loose for a crispier texture.

5. **Bake**  
   - Bake for **35–40 minutes**, or until the top is golden brown and the berry filling is bubbling around the edges.

6. **Serve**  
   - Let cool for a few minutes before serving.  
   - Enjoy warm with **custard**, **double cream**, or **vanilla ice cream**.

---

## 💡 Tips
- You can use any berries you like, or mix them with **apples**, **pears**, or **rhubarb**.  
- Add a pinch of **cinnamon** or **nutmeg** to the crumble for extra warmth.  
- If using frozen berries, no need to thaw — just add 1 extra teaspoon of cornstarch.
